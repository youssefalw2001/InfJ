# Manywise Product Direction

- Treat **Manywise // Room Zero** as a private-feeling, high-signal forum: dark technical surfaces, raw borders, dense thread indexes, server/channel hierarchy, status indicators, coded microcopy, and restrained lime/cyan/amber accents.
- The experience should feel younger and culturally online, closer to a well-run Discord server or underground technical forum than a wellness, therapy, lifestyle, or luxury editorial product.
- “Hacking forum” is a visual and cultural reference only. Never add cybercrime instruction, stolen data, credential trading, malware, evasion, or unauthorized-access features. Keep all technical content legitimate and safety-bounded.
- Build insider belonging through shared language, references, channel lore, pseudonyms, recurring members, and recognition. Never dehumanize outsiders or imply members are smarter, morally better, or the only real humans.
- The product remains an 18+ community. A minor-facing product would require separate youth-safety, consent, moderation, legal, and privacy work.
- Keep the original anime-inspired adult cast and Anime Analysis channel. Never copy or imply affiliation with anime franchises, studios, schools, or existing characters.
- Rarity language must remain accurate and non-hierarchical: some MBTI samples place INFJ around 1–2%, varying by sample, region, and method. Pair the signal with “rare does not mean ranked.”
- The Signal Calibration quiz routes members toward channels using declared preferences. It is optional, stores no hidden inference, and never verifies whether someone is a “real INFJ.”
- Preserve accessibility, privacy, server-side authorization, clear moderation, honest demo labels, keyboard access, readable contrast, reduced motion, and working controls beneath the more cryptic visual identity.
