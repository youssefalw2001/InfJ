# Manywise Product Direction

- Keep Manywise professional and emotionally intelligent while adding the energy of an after-school anime club: notebook details, club posters, character-study language, brighter editorial accents, and original illustrated cast members.
- The product remains an 18+ community. “High-school vibe” describes the nostalgic clubroom art direction, not a decision to admit minors. A minor-facing product would require separate youth-safety, consent, moderation, legal, and privacy work.
- Never copy or imply affiliation with anime franchises, studios, schools, or existing characters. Use only original characters and assets or clearly licensed material.
- Rarity language must be accurate and non-hierarchical. Say that some MBTI samples place INFJ around 1–2%, with results varying by sample, region, and method. Never present 1% as a universal scientific fact or proof that members are smarter, better, or more authentic.
- The “1–2% club” should feel like recognition and responsibility, not superiority or exclusion. Pair it with “rare does not mean ranked.”
- Quizzes should route members toward rooms and conversation styles using declared preferences. They must remain optional, store no hidden profile inference, and never claim to verify whether someone is a “real INFJ.”
- Anime discussion should focus on character arcs, symbolism, craft, emotional meaning, fandom friendship, and respectful disagreement. Avoid armchair diagnosis and deterministic typing of characters or members.
