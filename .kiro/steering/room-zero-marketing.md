# Manywise // Room Zero Marketing Studio

Apply these rules to every marketing-studio task and artifact.

## Canonical direction

- Read and follow `.kiro/steering/product-direction.md` before doing marketing work. If another brief conflicts with it, stop and identify the conflict.
- Treat every brief, calendar, concept, script, caption, experiment, video, and render as a draft until a human explicitly approves that exact version for publication.
- Never publish, post, schedule, upload, send, deploy, or trigger an external distribution workflow.
- Never access, request, accept, store, expose, or test social-media credentials, session tokens, API keys, cookies, or account recovery information.

## Content and research boundaries

- Use public sources and fictional material only. The sole non-public input allowed is sanitized, aggregate, non-identifying marketing analytics reviewed by `growth-loop`.
- Do not access private profiles, private groups, direct messages, paywalled or gated data, leaked data, or personal-level analytics. Do not bypass access controls or site restrictions.
- Learn from broad patterns rather than copying creators. Do not imitate a living creator's distinctive style, lift phrasing, reproduce protected characters, or imply affiliation with a franchise, studio, school, creator, or platform.
- Use only Manywise's original anime-inspired adult characters. When provenance or permission is unclear, flag the material and do not use it.
- Motion Lab may read the approved repository-original assets `public/after-class-cast.svg` and `public/icon.svg` for rendering, but must never edit them. It must not inspect other production content.
- Keep positioning explicitly 18+. Do not target minors or create minor-coded campaigns.
- Do not diagnose users, present MBTI as clinical fact, or make medical or mental-health claims.
- Do not claim INFJs or members are smarter, better, more human, more moral, or inherently superior. Do not demean outsiders.
- If a draft mentions INFJ rarity, state that some MBTI samples place INFJ around 1–2%, that results vary by sample, region, and method, and that rare does not mean ranked.
- Treat hacking as visual or cultural language only. Exclude offensive security instructions, malware, credential theft, evasion, stolen data, unauthorized access, and cybercrime promotion.
- Make drafts accessible: include caption and transcript plans for audiovisual work, meaningful on-screen text, readable contrast, reduced-motion handling, and plain-language alternatives where relevant.
- Substantiate factual claims. Separate sourced facts, observations, inferences, and creative proposals.

## Repository boundaries

- The six repo-local marketing agents must store new marketing work only under `marketing-studio/` and must never change production application behavior or files under `src/`, `public/`, `supabase/`, or deployment/runtime configuration.
- Marketing agents must never modify package manifests, lockfiles, dependency state, or repository-wide build configuration. A repository maintainer may make narrowly scoped package, workspace, lint, TypeScript, or ignore-file changes when a human has explicitly authorized building or maintaining the studio.
- Keep publication as a human-controlled step outside every agent workflow. Human approval of a draft does not authorize an agent to publish it.
